# Housing Cost Burden in California
Among California renter households, who spends too much of their income on housing, where is it most common, and what characteristics go with it?

## The questions for this study are:
1. **How common is it?** What share of renter households are cost burdened (30% or more of income on housing). What share of renter households are severely cost burdened (50% or more of income on housing). How does it vary by area?
2. **Who is affected?** How do the rates differ by income level, household size, the age of the head of household, and building type?
3. **What goes with severe burden?** Which characteristics tend to appear together with being severely burdened.
4. **Can a model flag high risk households?** Compare simple and more advanced models, with honest validation
5. **Where is it concentrated?** A ranking of areas, with uncertainty.
6. **What can this data not show?** The limits

## Definition of terms
In this study, **Cost burdened** means a renter household that spends 30% or more but less than 50% of his/her income on housing. **Severely cost burdened** means a renter household that spends 50% or more of his/her income on housing.
